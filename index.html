Questions

Before you start.

Is Tsundoku only for physical books?
No. Organize physical books, ebooks, audiobooks, and even books you've read but don't own.
Can I organize books however I want?
Absolutely. Create custom shelves, browse by author, filter by reading status, and organize your collection the way your mind naturally works.
Can I share my library?
Yes. Create a public profile and share your bookshelf with anyone — no account required to view it.
Why is it called Tsundoku?
Tsundoku is a Japanese word describing the habit of buying books and letting them pile up before reading them. We think that's part of being a reader — not something to feel guilty about.
Is it free?
Yes. You can start building your personal library for free, with no credit card required.

chapter 01 — the word

a japanese word for a very good habit.

tsundoku is what happens when 積む — to pile up — meets 読む — to read. it means owning more books than you've read. and here's the secret: that's not failure. that's a library in progress.

01buying books and reading books are two different hobbies — and both are wonderful.
02an unread book is a promise, not a debt. it will wait for you. they always do.
03the pile by your bed says you believe in a future where you have time to read. keep believing.
✓ no guilt. ever. that's a design rule, not a slogan.
chapter 02 — the idea

the app is just a photograph of your shelf.

your physical collection stays the hero — the real thing, on a real shelf, in your real home. tsundoku simply keeps a quiet copy: every cover, every status, every reason you bought it. watch the pile move in.

chapter 07 — where it lives

the shelf stays physical.
the app just keeps the memory.

tsundoku will never ask you to read faster, catch up, or clear the pile. it holds the photograph so you can keep the real thing — dusty, loved, and entirely yours.
